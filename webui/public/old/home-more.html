This laboratory, managed by Associate Professor James Trevelyan has been founded to diversify the robotics research interests of the Department of Mechanical and Materials Engineering. The primary objective is to provide support for robotics teaching and research. The laboratory is available to research staff, postgraduate and undergraduate students.
