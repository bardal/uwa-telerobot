Australia's Telerobot Wins JARA Award

The 1996 JARA AWARDS are presented to the winners at the Awards Ceremony during the 27th International Symposium on Industrial Robots in Milan, Italy on 6 Oct 1996.

Japan Industrial Robot Association (renamed as the Japan Robot Association - JARA) established JIRA (JARA) AWARDS in 1989, to celebrate the 20th anniversary of ISIR in TOKYO and wish it further success in the future by awarding excellent papers presented at the ISIR with honor.The 1996 JARA AWARDS are the eighth presented.

JARA AWARDS are given every year to the most outstanding papers in three fields: Research and Development, Application Technology and Management which were presented at the ISIR in the previous year.

In the field of Management, the JARA AWARD is presented to Mr. Kenneth Taylor and Mr James Trevelyan from the University of Western Australia for their paper entitled, "Australia's Telerobot On The Web".

The paper reports an experimental project that has opened research facilities to the users of the Internet computer network by means of letting them remotely control an industrial robot through the World Wide Web. This project has proved the effectiveness of a new-style use of facilities for research and training related to robots. Furthermore, this project should be highly evaluated because it offered the chance for many people to use a robot that is not otherwise frequently used, aiming for promotion of diffusion of industrial robots and effective use of research resources. From this view point, this paper has been chosen for the JARA AWARD in the field of management.

Japan Robot Association.

Chairman, Seiuemon Inaba (Chairman '96 JARA AWARDS Executive Committee)
