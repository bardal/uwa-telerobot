Competition Results

Winner Bernaut Plana

The interface design competition closed on 1 October 1997 and there were three entries. It is apparent that designing an interface requires a lot of time and thought so thank you to the three people who entered. We are intending to try out some of the ideas from the competition entries and the UWA student teams in the default pages and by comparing operator actions with those made using the current interface quantify the effect of some of the changes. The winner was Bernat Plana. The judges were Ken Taylor and Barney Dalton and some comments on the three submissions are made below. All three designs aimed to cater for both new and experienced operators and minimised scrolling. The methods adopted to achieve this were varied.

 Interface template jvp3 by Joe Petsche

,

 The report and explanation of what was done was excellent. The observer page gives the impression that image controls and robot position can be controlled but this is not possible. There is a lot of unused space on the screen that the operator sees, for example the "controller" label has been made small but the additional space made available has not been used for other things. Making the chat applet an optional extra is probably a good idea as it is not widely used and if used from the operators page requires logging in again after each move. Constructions are fastest with the multiple move method so the idea of placing most emphasis on this seems worthwhile. Only a small percentage of operators use this technique at present so we intend to investigate ways of increasing its use. The first page received provided a good introduction.

Interface template kevin5 by Kevin Christ

,

 The observer page again has controls that can't have any effect. Specifically camera zoom and camera off controls. This information is additional to that on the default page but could have been conveyed in a textual form rather than controls so that it was obvious that it could not be altered. The idea of associating controls with the relevant image is a worthwhile one but the actual associations used are confusing. The x and y controls are both associated with cameras looking from close to the same direction. The first page received provided a good introduction.

 Winning interface template plana by Bernat Plana

 The changes to the observer page provide a colourful page that draws the eye to the central features. This design has the highest information density without scrolling. The New controller page provides a good introduction which is visible while looking at the images and controls, bullet points helping to draw attention to explanatory text. The controller page is extremely clear and well set out, different coloured tables helping to demarcate seperate parts of the interface. Contrary to expectation large image sizes do not upset the structure of the page too much, although it may be worth having spin and tilt on a second row. One possible critisism is that the image clicking method of movement is not an obvious option.

This design was selected as the winner due to its high information density, simplicity of operation, and use of colour to emphasise the key aspects of the page.
