How To Move Blocks!!

The first two pictures are from cameras at 45 degrees to each other. X increases to the right and Y increases away (in the first two images). The grid is 100mm spacing, positions are in mm. To stand a block up, pick it up with a tilt of -45, then go up and rotate to a tilt of +45. If it doesn't move you've probably reached a joint angle limitation, try going in a different direction, or if you're trying to do a rotation, go to a different point on the table first.

If you want a faster response turn some of the cameras off.

The X and Y axis are marked on the table, Z goes up from the table (you cannot go below 7). The grid lines are 100mm apart and positions are in mm.
