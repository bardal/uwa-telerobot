  • The grid is 100mm SQUARES.The X and Y axis are marked on the table, Z goes up from the table (for Z > 7mm).
  • To move the robot, either enter values in the boxes, or use your mouse by clicking on one of the first two camera images.
  • To stand a block up, pick it up with a tilt of -45, then go up and rotate to a tilt of +45. See the Gripper Orientation Index for example pictures.

    You have exclusive control if you use the robot within 3 minutes. You have made MoveCountVal moves, and have been on since StartTimeVal. Your last move was made at LastTimeVal.
