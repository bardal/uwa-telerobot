The two left hand images are from cameras at 45 degrees to each other. These images are calibrated, i.e. by clicking on first one then the other image you can define a point in space to go to. The X and Y axis are marked on the table, Z goes up from the table (you cannot go below 7). The grid lines are 100mm apart and positions are in mm. To stand a block up, pick it up with a tilt of -45, then go up and rotate to a tilt of +45. If you want a faster response turn some of the cameras off. If it doesn't move you've probably reached a joint angle limitation, try going in a different direction, or if you're trying to do a rotation, go to a different point on the table first.
