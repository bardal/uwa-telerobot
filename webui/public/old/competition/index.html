Overview

Implement the best idea to improve the telerobot operator interface before 1 October 1997 and you will get the US$100 prize. Read how to do it here. The prize goes to the person who implements the best ideas.

We get a lot of comments suggesting ways that the telerobot interface could be improved and it is apparent that the interface layout significantly affects operator behavior and satisfaction. For example the graph below shows operator satisfaction as measured by moves per session with different interfaces.

Quite small changes can influence the frequency that a particular command is used and some statistical information is shown here. The goals, some of which are contradictory, are to make it simple, fast, comprehensive and provide a sense of presence.

How To Enter

Prepare and submit a template. Try it out yourself and modify it until you are happy with it. Every modification will require a new template name. Then leave a message stating the name of your template and say a little about why it is an improvement. If you can provide references to any human computer interaction or telerobotic research you were able to apply this can add weight to your argument.

After the competition is closed we will judge who has provided the best ideas and award the prize which will be announced on this site. The criteria is not the best design but the best ideas presented. So if someone copies your idea and improves on it marginally your effort will be judged superior.

Rules

  1. The competition is not open to students or employees of the University of Western Australia.
  2. The judges decision will be final.
  3. The competition will close on 1 October 1997.
  4. The prize will be paid to the person whose name and email address are submitted with the name of a design in the comments either by cheque (if within Australia), International Money Order or bank transfer. It will be paid in a convenient currency at the prevailing exchange rate.
  5. Multiple entries are allowed.
