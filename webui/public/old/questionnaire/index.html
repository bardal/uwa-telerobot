Telerobot User Questionaire

WE NEED YOUR HELP!!

This site has been created to research potential uses for telerobotics. In order to gain a better understanding of how to implement telerobots on this and other sites we would like some feedback from you, the users.

We would like to know a bit about yourself, what you think of the telerobot, and how it can be improved.

NOTE: Any information you supply will be kept totally confidential and not passed on to other parties.

Registered Users can log into the telerobot with a higher level of access to guests. If you want to become a registered user, you must provide an email address so your telerobot PIN can be sent to you be email.

We also appreciate the input of those who don't want to become registered users. If this is you, then uncheck the checkbox below and you will not be registered, so no email address is required.

Register me and send my PIN by e-mail.

If you are registering, please choose a UserID. This is what you type in when you log in. (We suggest you keep it less than 15 characters.)
