Driving the robot

Commands are GBH path scripts. A script is a list of waypoints separated by ;; each waypoint sets one or more axes separated by ,:

x y zgripper position in mm (table corner is the origin, z up; z must be at least 5)
sspin in degrees (-90 points the gripper along +x)
ttilt in degrees, or tf / tv / tb for -45 / 0 / 45
g0 / g1open / close the gripper (also o / c)
rbefore an axis: relative to the current position, e.g. rz-20

Example: pick up the red block and stack it on the blue one:

x120,y150,z60,s-90,t0,g0;z15;g1;z80;x330,y120;z45;g0;z90

The first waypoint opens the gripper, which must be open to grip. The gripper only grips with the tilt within ±20°. Blocks stay where they are left until the lab is restarted.

The camera images carry their calibration, so the 3D stick cursor drawn over them can be dragged to choose a position: drag the top marker to change height, the bottom marker or the plumb line to move across the table, and the needles to change spin and tilt.
